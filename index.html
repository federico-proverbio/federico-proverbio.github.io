Hello!

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München (LMU). My research interests lie on quantitative macroeconomic modelling. I want to understand how policies affect heterogenous households along the income distribution and the life cycle.

Before starting my PhD, I completed an M.Sc. in Economics at the University of Bonn and a B.Sc. in Business Administration and Economics at Tor Vergata University of Rome. My master's thesis studied whether housing policy differences can explain homeownership gaps between Germany and Italy in an overlapping-generations model.

You can find more detail on my research projects on the Research page, and my full academic history on my CV page.
